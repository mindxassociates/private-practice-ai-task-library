A simple workflow

Delegate with intention.

AI agents can draft, sort, summarize, and prepare. You define boundaries, check the output, and decide what is ready to leave the room.

01 / SELECT

Pick a contained task

Choose something repeatable with a clear finish line, such as formatting a blog draft or preparing a checklist.

02 / BRIEF

Give context and limits

State the audience, desired format, source material, what must stay private, and what the agent must not do.

03 / REVIEW

Check before it counts

Verify facts, tone, links, policy alignment, and any action with financial, clinical, or client impact.

Designed for clinical practices

Keep the right work with the right reviewer.

Tools differ in how they store and use data. Check the product settings and your professional, contractual, and legal obligations before sharing information.

🔒

Protect client information

Do not paste identifiable client details, records, messages, or session notes into a general-purpose AI agent. Use approved systems and de-identified inputs where permitted.

🧭

Keep clinical decisions human

Agents may help format or organize clinician-authored materials. They should not diagnose, assess risk, recommend treatment, or communicate clinical guidance on your behalf.

✅

Require approval for actions

Use drafts and previews for billing, scheduling, contracts, public posts, emails, and account changes. A responsible human should review and authorize each consequential action.

This resource is general operational guidance, not legal, compliance, or clinical advice. Confirm that your chosen tools and workflows meet your practice requirements.
